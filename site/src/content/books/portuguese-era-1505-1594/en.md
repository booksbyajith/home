**ලංකාවේ පෘතුගීසි සිටි යුගය (1505-1594)** (*The Portuguese Period in Lanka, 1505–1594*) is a research work, written in Sinhala, on the period of Portuguese presence in Sri Lanka between 1505 and 1594.

The book was launched on 11 November 2026 at the Mahaweli Centre in Colombo.
