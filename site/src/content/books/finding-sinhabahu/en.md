**Finding Sinhabahu** analyses the early history of Sri Lanka as told in the ancient chronicles, using evidence from inscriptions, archaeology, anthropology and genetics.

Published by Vijitha Yapa Publishers, the book won the State Literary Award 2020 in the Social Science category.
