Welcome to this website.

Dr. Ajith Amarasinghe is a medical specialist by profession and a member of the Royal Asiatic Society of Sri Lanka. Drawing on the chronicles, inscriptions, foreign records and his own field journeys, he revisits forgotten chapters of Lankan history.

## His books

- **[ලක් ඉතිහාසයෙන් වසන් වූ චීන මෙහෙයුම](../../books/chinese-expedition/)** (*The Chinese Expedition Hidden from Lankan History*, 2014) — the story of the 15th-century expedition of the Chinese admiral Zheng He and the capture of King Vira Alakesvara.
- **[Finding Sinhabahu](../../books/finding-sinhabahu/)** — an analysis of the early history of Sri Lanka using inscriptions, archaeology, anthropology and genetics, recognised at the 2020 State Literary Awards.
- **[ලංකාවේ පෘතුගීසි සිටි යුගය (1505-1594)](../../books/portuguese-era-1505-1594/)** (*The Portuguese in Sri Lanka*) — his latest work, launched on 11 November 2026 at the Mahaweli Centre, Colombo.

Come back for new writing, book launches and stories from Lankan history.
