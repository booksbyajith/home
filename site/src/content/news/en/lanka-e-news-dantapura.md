On 17 April 2025, on the eve of the exposition of the Sacred Tooth Relic in Kandy, *Lanka-e-News* published a feature by Sujeewa Senarath on Dantapura in ancient Kalinga, from where the chronicles say the Tooth Relic was brought to Sri Lanka.

The article explains that in *Finding Sinhabahu* Dr. Ajith Amarasinghe identifies the site, in two chapters spanning 52 pages, as present-day Dantapuram near Srikakulam in northern Andhra Pradesh, which he visited in July 2015. It describes the Buddhist ruins, stupas and the ancient earthen city wall still found in the area.
