On 5 November 2026, *The Lanka Leader* announced the launch of Dr. Ajith Amarasinghe's new research book, *The Portuguese Period in Sri Lanka (1505–1594)*, held on 11 November at the Mahaweli Centre, Ananda Coomaraswamy Mawatha, Colombo 07.

The article describes the book as the most extensive and detailed work written on this period, one that places known historical information in the correct chronological order and questions several long-accepted conclusions about the Portuguese presence in Sri Lanka. It also notes that Prof. Nirmal Ranjith Dewasiri and Prof. Karunaratne Paranawithana would speak at the launch.
