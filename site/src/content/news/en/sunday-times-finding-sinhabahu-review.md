On 21 April 2019, *The Sunday Times* published Tissa Devendra's review of *Finding Sinhabahu* (Vijitha Yapa Publications), under the title "A search for Vijaya's origins".

The review describes how the book uses Indian and other records, together with the author's own exploration, to identify Sinhapura, the capital of Sinhabahu, and argues that most early Indo-Aryan-speaking settlers came from the regions now covered by Orissa and Bengal. The reviewer notes that the findings rest on documentary, archaeological, linguistic and genetic evidence, and calls the book "an altogether fascinating foray into a little studied aspect of the history of the Sinhala people and their distant roots".
