Dr. Ajith Amarasinghe's latest research work, **ලංකාවේ පෘතුගීසි සිටි යුගය (1505-1594)** (*The Portuguese in Sri Lanka, 1505–1594*), was launched on 11 November 2026 at the Mahaweli Centre in Colombo.

Read the press coverage of the launch on [The Leader](https://theleader.lk/news/dr-ajith-amarasinghe-launches-book-on-portuguese-era).
