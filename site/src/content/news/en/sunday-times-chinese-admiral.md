On 8 March 2015, *The Sunday Times* published an interview by Kumudini Hettiarachchi with Dr. Ajith Amarasinghe about the research behind *ලක් ඉතිහාසයෙන් වසන් වූ චීන මෙහෙයුම* (*The Chinese Expedition Hidden from Lankan History*).

The article follows his search through Kotte, Jaffna and as far as Taiwan for the story of the 15th-century confrontation between the Chinese Admiral Zheng He and Vira Alakesvara, in which a king of southern Ceylon was captured and taken to the Ming court in Nanjing.
