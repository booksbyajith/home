   - Branch: <span data-config-text="bank-branch">…</span>, Sri Lanka
3. Send a copy of your receipt along with your name and the postal address to the WhatsApp number <a data-config="whatsapp" href="../../contact/"><span data-wa-label>WhatsApp</span></a>.
4. You will receive a message once the books you ordered have been posted.
