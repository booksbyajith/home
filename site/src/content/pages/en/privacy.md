This policy explains how we handle personal information when you order a book, track an order, subscribe to updates or contact us through this website.

## What we collect and why

- **When you order:** your name, mobile and WhatsApp numbers, email address (optional), delivery address, district, postal code, landmark or delivery notes, any gift or dedication request, the books you order, and a photo or PDF of your bank slip. We use these only to verify your payment, prepare and deliver your order, and contact you about it.
- **Tracking an order:** we ask for your order number and the last four digits of your mobile number. The tracking page shows only the order status and courier details, never your name, address or phone number.
- **Updates, stock alerts, reviews and bulk enquiries:** the details you type into those forms, with your consent.

## On your device

While you fill in the order form, your answers and your bank slip are saved in your own browser so you do not lose them if the page reloads. They are deleted from your browser when your order is sent, or when you choose "Start over". A short list of your orders (order number, date, books, last four digits of your phone) and your receipt (which also shows your name and delivery address) stay in your browser so you can track and download them later. You can remove them from the tracking page.

## Who can see your information

Only the small team that processes orders can see order details. Your information is stored securely with Google Firebase in data centres in the Asia-Pacific region. It is never sold or shared for marketing. We share your name, phone number and address with the courier only to deliver your book. Order emails are sent through our email provider, and if you message us, WhatsApp and Facebook handle those messages under their own policies.

## Analytics and cookies

If analytics are switched on, we use a privacy-friendly service that does not use cookies and records only which pages are visited. We do not use advertising cookies unless you accept them in a banner shown for that purpose.

## How long we keep it

We keep order details for as long as needed to deliver the order and handle any problems. Personal details of completed, rejected or cancelled orders (name, phone numbers, email, address, dedication note and the bank slip) are removed after a period set by us, normally 12 months. The order number, books, amounts and district are kept for our accounts. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

## Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us on WhatsApp or through our Facebook page. You can unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email.
