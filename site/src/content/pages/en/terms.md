## Ordering and payment

Orders are confirmed once we have verified your bank transfer against the slip you uploaded. Prices and delivery fees are shown in Sri Lankan rupees before you pay. If the total we calculate differs from what you saw, we will contact you before dispatch.

## Delivery

We deliver within Sri Lanka by post or courier. Books are usually posted within two working days of payment verification and normally arrive within three days of posting. Delivery times can be longer during holidays or in remote areas. We send you the courier name and tracking number on WhatsApp, and you can follow the order on the tracking page.

## Pre-orders

Pre-ordered books are posted as soon as they are available. We will tell you the expected date when you order.

## Damaged copies

If a book arrives damaged or with a printing fault, send us a photo and your order number on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery. We will send a replacement at no cost.

## Cancellations and refunds

You can cancel an order before it is posted. If you have already paid, we refund the full amount to your bank account. If we cannot verify a payment, we will contact you; if the matter cannot be resolved, any amount received is refunded.

## Contact

For any question about an order, contact us on WhatsApp with your order number.
