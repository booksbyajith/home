Dr. Ajith Amarasinghe is a medical specialist by profession and a researcher of Sri Lankan history. He is a member of the Royal Asiatic Society of Sri Lanka and the author of three historical research works.

His book *Finding Sinhabahu* won the State Literary Award 2020 in the Social Science category. His latest work, *ලංකාවේ පෘතුගීසි සිටි යුගය (1505-1594)*, was launched in November 2026.
